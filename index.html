How we look at every trade

The same five steps each time. It keeps us calm when the market isn't.

  1. Map

    Mark the key levels and the trend before looking at any entry.

  2. Wait

    Let price reach the area you marked. Skipping a trade is fine.

  3. Execute

    Enter only when entry, stop and target are written down.

  4. Manage

    Keep each position small enough that one loss doesn't hurt much.

  5. Review

    Look back at wins and losses. That's where most of the learning is.
